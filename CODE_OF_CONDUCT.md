# Len Community Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Len's community should be respectful, patient, privacy-conscious, and focused on improving the software. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and defects without attacking people;
- welcome questions and differing levels of experience;
- give specific, constructive feedback;
- respect personal boundaries and the project's non-clinical scope; and
- protect other people's privacy, especially health and identity information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, pressuring anyone to disclose health information, presenting repository discussion as individualized medical advice, or encouraging self-harm or dangerous conduct.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository and in public or private interactions undertaken on behalf of the project. It does not make maintainers clinicians, crisis responders, or emergency monitors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, or other participation that violates this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and centered on community safety.

Report a conduct concern privately to `projectmentalhealthtracker@gmail.com` with only the minimum information needed to understand the repository interaction. Do not include medical records, assessment responses, generated Len files, credentials, or unrelated personal information. Suspected software vulnerabilities belong in [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/DScannellWiz/len-mental-health/security/advisories/new), not in a conduct report.

The project address is not continuously monitored and is not a medical, crisis, or emergency channel. If immediate danger or urgent help is involved, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource.
